# Code of conduct

pdf2epub-kit is a small project for authors, publishers and readers. We want everyone who takes part to feel welcome and safe.

## Our standards

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are authors, not programmers.
- Discuss ideas and code, not people. Disagree openly, but respectfully.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or sexualised language or imagery, in any form.
- Respect privacy: never post someone else's personal data, and never share purchased books, PDFs, EPUBs or the buyer details stamped in them (see [docs/privacy.md](docs/privacy.md)).
- Respect authors' rights: don't use the project to share books you have no rights to.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone breaks these rules, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub ([@5aahil](https://github.com/5aahil)). Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and block people who break these rules, whatever the severity. A first, minor problem usually gets a private note. Repeated or serious problems lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
